Our Story

A passion for exceptional coffee, community, and craft.

Welcome to Viva's Cafe

Founded in 2021, Viva's Cafe began as a humble dream to bring world-class coffee craftsmanship and a welcoming space to our local neighborhood.

Every cup we serve is sourced from fair-trade farmers across South America and East Africa. Our roasters obsess over flavor profiles to highlight the rich, unique notes of each origin.

Whether you're grabbing your morning espresso on the go or sitting down for a relaxed brunch with friends, Viva's is designed to be your home away from home.
